## Decisions made

- **Dormancy convention** (user asked, then approved): a live application quiet
  for 21 days is flagged "Follow up", 45 days "Presumed closed" — derived at
  render time, **never written back as a status**. Silence and a stated decision
  are different facts, and conflating them would corrupt the funnel figures.
  Thresholds are conventions, not fitted values; revisit after 4–5 outcomes.
- **Charts stay above the table.** They were briefly reordered (table first) to
  address a reported "page is cut off"; the user pushed back and it was reverted.
- The reported truncation was **the preview pane's frame**, not the document —
  confirmed by auditing every height/overflow rule and by opening in a real
  browser. Recorded as a memory so it isn't re-diagnosed as a layout problem.
